Best Free PDF Readers for 2024

By PDF Converter Team • 7 min read

Your web browser (Chrome, Edge) is fine for quick viewing, but if you work with PDFs daily, you need a dedicated reader. The good news? You don't need to pay for Adobe Acrobat Pro.

Here are the best free PDF readers available today that are lightweight, feature-rich, and user-friendly.

1. Adobe Acrobat Reader DC (The Standard)

Pros: It is the industry standard. It handles forms and digital signatures better than anything else. If a government form won't open in Chrome, it will open here.

Cons: It is heavy, often slow to load, and constantly nags you to upgrade to the paid version.

2. Foxit Reader (The Power User Choice)

Foxit has been the top alternative to Adobe for years. It is lightweight and includes features that Adobe charges for, like creating PDFs from other files.

Best for: Users who want a ribbon-style interface (like Microsoft Word) and robust annotation tools.

3. SumatraPDF (The Minimalist)

If you hate bloatware, SumatraPDF is for you. It is open-source and incredibly fast. It strips away all the editing features and focuses purely on reading.

Best for: Reading eBooks or large manuals on older computers.

4. Microsoft Edge (The Surprise Contender)

Yes, the web browser. Microsoft has invested heavily in Edge's PDF engine. It now includes drawing tools, highlighting, and even a "Read Aloud" feature that is surprisingly natural.

Best for: Students who need to listen to their textbooks.

Conclusion

For viewing, stick with your browser or SumatraPDF. For filling out complex forms, keep Adobe Reader installed as a backup. You likely do not need to spend money on premium software just to read a document.
