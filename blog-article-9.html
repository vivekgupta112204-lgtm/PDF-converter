Legal Validity of Digital Documents

By PDF Converter Team • 8 min read

One of the most common questions we get is: "If I scan a signed contract and turn it into a PDF, does it still count?"

The short answer is almost always Yes. But there are important nuances to understand about digital document legality in 2024.

The ESIGN Act and Global Standards

In the United States, the ESIGN Act of 2000 granted electronic signatures the same legal status as handwritten ones. Similar laws exist globally, such as eIDAS in the EU.

This means a contract sent as a PDF via email is just as binding as one sent via FedEx.

Scanned Copies vs. Original Digital Files

There is a technical difference between a "wet signature" that is scanned and an "electronic signature" applied digitally.

  • Scanned Wet Signature: You print, sign with a pen, scan it back to PDF. This is widely accepted for most business agreements.
  • Digital Signature: You use software to apply a cryptographic signature. This provides higher security because it tracks exactly who signed and when.

Why PDF is the Legal Standard

Courts and governments prefer PDF (specifically PDF/A for archiving) because it is an immutable format.

If you send a contract as a Word document, the other party could accidentally delete a paragraph or change a number. A PDF locks the content in place, providing a reliable record of what was agreed upon.

Best Practice for Legal Scanning

If you are digitizing legal documents using our JPG to PDF tool:

  1. Ensure the lighting is even and the text is legible.
  2. Include all pages, even blank ones, to prove nothing was omitted.
  3. Keep the original paper copy if required by specific local laws (e.g., for wills or deeds).
