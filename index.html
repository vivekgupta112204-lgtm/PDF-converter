Why Convert Images to PDF?

Converting images (JPG, PNG) to PDF is essential for professional documentation. PDFs maintain consistent formatting across all devices, reduce file sizes for easier sharing via email, and are the global standard for printing and archiving. Whether you are a student submitting homework or a business professional sending receipts, a PDF ensures your document looks exactly as intended.
