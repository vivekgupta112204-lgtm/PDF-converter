How to Digitally Sign a PDF

By PDF Converter Team • 6 min read

The days of "Print > Sign > Scan > Email" are over. If you are still using a physical pen to sign digital contracts, you are wasting time and paper.

Digital signatures are secure, legally binding, and incredibly fast. In this guide, we'll show you how to sign a PDF on any device.

Electronic Signature vs. Digital Signature

First, a quick distinction.

  • Electronic Signature: An image of your signature placed on a document. Fast and easy.
  • Digital Signature: A cryptographic mark that verifies your identity and ensures the document hasn't been altered. More secure.

For most routine business (leases, permission slips, invoices), a standard electronic signature is sufficient.

Method 1: Using Adobe Acrobat Reader (Free)

Adobe Reader is the industry standard. It has a built-in "Fill & Sign" feature.

  1. Open your PDF in Adobe Reader.
  2. Click the Fill & Sign icon in the right pane.
  3. Select "Sign yourself".
  4. You can draw your signature with your mouse, type it, or upload an image of your signature.
  5. Place the signature anywhere on the page and save.

Method 2: Mac Preview (Built-in)

Mac users don't even need Adobe. The "Preview" app is powerful.

  1. Open the PDF in Preview.
  2. Click the Markup Toolbar button (looks like a pen tip).
  3. Click the Signature icon.
  4. Cool Tip: You can sign a piece of white paper and hold it up to your webcam. Preview will scan it and turn it into a transparent signature automatically!

Method 3: Signing on Mobile

Sometimes you are on the go.

  • iOS: Open the PDF from Mail or Files. Tap the "Markup" icon (pen). Tap "+" and choose "Signature". Sign with your finger.
  • Android: Use the Adobe Fill & Sign app or Google Drive's markup features.

What if I have an image of a contract?

If someone sent you a photo of a contract (JPG), you can't sign it easily. Use our Image to PDF tool first to convert it into a standard document, then apply your signature using the methods above.
