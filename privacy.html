Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. No Server Uploads

We prioritize your security above all else. Unlike many other online tools, we do not upload your files to our servers. All file processing is performed locally within your web browser using client-side WebAssembly technology. Your documents never leave your device.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No user accounts are required to use this service.
  • No email addresses are collected.
  • No file contents are viewed, analyzed, or stored by us.

3. File Retention

Since files are processed exclusively on your device, we have absolutely no access to them. Once you close the browser tab or refresh the page, any temporary data used for the conversion is instantly cleared from your browser's memory.

4. Cookies & Usage Data

We may use standard third-party services (such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense) to analyze website traffic and display advertisements. These services may collect anonymous data (e.g., browser type, visit duration, geographic location) to improve user experience, but they cannot identify you personally or access your files.
