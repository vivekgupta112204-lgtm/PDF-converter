Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Introduction

Your privacy is critically important to us. At PDF Converter, we have a few fundamental principles:

  • We do not ask you for personal information unless we truly need it.
  • We do not share your personal information with anyone except to comply with the law, develop our products, or protect our rights.
  • We do not store personal information on our servers unless required for the on-going operation of one of our services.

2. File Security (Client-Side Processing)

We pride ourselves on being a secure, client-side application. Unlike other converters that upload your files to a cloud server to be processed:

Your files never leave your device.

All conversion logic (JPG to PDF) happens within your web browser using WebAssembly and JavaScript technologies. This means we technically cannot see, store, copy, or share your documents because they are never transmitted to us.

3. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union, we adhere to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect or process personal data or files on our servers, we act as a tool provider rather than a data processor. However, we respect your rights to privacy and data control.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies to help identify and track visitors, their usage of our website, and their website access preferences. Visitors who do not wish to have cookies placed on their computers should set their browsers to refuse cookies before using our website.

We may use third-party services like Google Analytics or Google AdSense. These third parties may access your data and are subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective when they are posted on this page.
