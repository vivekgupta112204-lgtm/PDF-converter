Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. No Server Uploads

We prioritize your security. Unlike other online converters, we do not upload your files to our servers. All file processing is performed locally within your web browser using client-side technologies (WebAssembly/JavaScript). Your documents never leave your device.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No user accounts required.
  • No email addresses collected.
  • No file contents viewed or stored.

3. File Retention

Since files are processed on your device, we have no access to them. Once you close the browser tab or refresh the page, any temporary data used for the conversion is instantly cleared from your browser's memory.

4. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We use third-party services like Google Analytics to understand website traffic. These services may collect anonymous data (e.g., browser type, visit duration) but cannot identify you personally or access your files.
